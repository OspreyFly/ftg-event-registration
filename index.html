🎃 Contest Rules 🎃

Read the rules before entering the carving crypt. A fair fright for everyone!

⏳60 minutes to carve your masterpiece!🎃
1

Each contestant will have 60 minutes to complete their pumpkin.

2

FTG will provide the pumpkins. Contestants will draw numbers to determine the order of pumpkin selection.

3

All carving must take place during the designated contest period. No pre-carved pumpkins are permitted.

4

Contestants may bring their own carving tools or use the basic equipment provided by FTG.

5

Power tools, chainsaws, and other hazardous equipment are prohibited.

6

Pumpkins must be carved. Painting alone does not qualify.

7

Paint, markers, and small decorations are allowed, provided the pumpkin is primarily carved.

8

Printed templates, stencils, and reference images are allowed.

9

Each pumpkin must have an opening or design that allows light from an LED candle to shine through.

10

All carving must stop when the 60-minute timer expires.

11

Contestants must clean their workstations after carving.
